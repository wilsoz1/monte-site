Monte offers an optional Daily Text Brief: one short text message each morning summarizing what's left to spend, upcoming bills, and your next money move.

Program description

  • Opt-in: in the Monte iOS app, under Settings → Notifications → Daily Text Brief, you enter your phone number, choose a delivery time, and enable the toggle. The program is off by default and consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Message frequency: 1 message per day.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel immediately, or turn off "Daily Text Brief" in the app. After you send STOP, you'll receive one final message confirming your opt-out. Reply HELP for help at any time, or contact zachwilson106@gmail.com.

Privacy

Your phone number is used solely to deliver the messages you requested. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
