It texts you first.

A morning brief with what you can safely spend, and alerts the moment something needs you — a bill jump, a budget about to blow, a paycheck landing. Reply by text; Monte answers from your real numbers.

It plans your cash.

Every bill on its real date, a year of cash flow ahead, and a warning before the dip — so the car payment on the 13th never collides with a good intention.

It orders your dollars.

One standing order for your next dollar — cash cushion, then the debt snowball, then wealth. Monte shows its math, tracks the streak, and hands you a gem when a step falls.
